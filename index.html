01 About

I'm a law library professional at the University of Missouri School of Law, where I earned my J.D. in 2026 and am now pursuing a Master of Library and Information Science. Day to day, I lead our reference desk, help pro se patrons, students, faculty, and attorneys find what they need, and build research guides for people starting out in an area of law.

Before that, I clerked in civil litigation, summered at a regional firm, and helped edit the Journal of Dispute Resolution. I came to law from religious studies, which taught me to read closely and argue carefully. I also write a little code, mostly to make information easier to search and see.
