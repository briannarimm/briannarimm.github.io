I built this for legal and academic writing. Drag symbols into the editor or type them. You get LaTeX, plain text, a Word equation and an image. The logic tools check arguments, rules and duties as you write them.

01Build

Click a symbol to insert it, or drag it to a spot in the editor. Type / for a fraction, ^ for a power and \ for any command.
