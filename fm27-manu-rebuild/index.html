Signing rules that shape everything

At a glance

  • A foreign signing needs 15 work-permit (GBE) points, or one of at most four ESC places.
  • ESC places are only for players born on or after January 1, 2003.
  • Foreign under-18s cannot join you; they can sign at 18 and still become homegrown.
  • On day one, read the Premier League rules screen for your ESC places and the Squad Cost Ratio wording.

What FM27 changed C: work permits follow the new government and FA guidelines, with bands regraded; ESC permits are limited to under-23s, the rules screen shows the cut-off birth date, and existing holders keep theirs for that season; Squad Cost Ratio rules were added.

What this means for you

  • Brazil is the easiest non-European source: a Série A regular banks 10 points from the club band alone.
  • Argentina, Mexico and MLS (Band 3) and Colombia and Japan (Band 4) work for players with minutes or caps.
  • Africa-based prospects sit in Band 6. Expect to need senior caps for a highly ranked nation, an ESC place, or a prior move to a Band 1-3 league.
  • Do not promise "regular starter" to a 17-year-old, or treat unused wage room as free to spend before reading the Squad Cost Ratio rule.

The primary tactic, "Heavy Metal"

At a glance

  • The model is Klopp's Liverpool of 2018-20: a 4-3-3 that becomes a 2-3-5 on the ball and swarms the moment the ball is lost.
  • Style Gegenpress, Mentality Positive, 4-3-3 DM Wide in both phases.
  • Seven role changes from the preset: striker, left wide forward, right No. 8, and off the ball the left full-back, one centre-back, the holding midfielder and one No. 8.
  • Tempo and Trigger Press each sit one notch below the preset on purpose, to protect condition.
  • Leave Pressing Trap on Balanced; it is the one instruction with test evidence against changing it.

The model in one paragraph. Both full-backs went high and wide and the front three played narrow. Alexander-Arnold created with passes from deep, Robertson with overlapping runs. Fabinho anchored; Henderson and Wijnaldum ran, covered the space behind the full-backs and kept the ball moving. Firmino dropped to link, Salah (left-footed, right side) scored, and Mané (right-footed, left side) ran in behind and tracked harder. Without the ball Salah and Mané pressed the centre-backs from outside in, Firmino sat on the opposition pivot, the midfield jumped onto the full-backs, and the two No. 8s were the second wave of the counter-press. Behind them: a very high line, van Dijk covering beside a quick partner, Alisson sweeping.

What the engine rewards, the physical cost and rotation

At a glance

  • Early community testing says athletes win: Acceleration and Pace first, then Jumping Reach, Stamina, Anticipation, Work Rate.
  • Condition and sharpness are worth more points than any single instruction, so rotation is your biggest lever.
  • Gegenpress was the best-scoring preset in that testing, and edited tactics beat presets.
  • Do not start anyone below about 90% condition unless the game is decisive.
  • Carry 22-24 senior outfield pressers and three goalkeepers.

Rotation rules of thumb.

  • Nobody starts below about 90% condition unless the game is decisive, and fringe players get a start every two to three weeks to hold sharpness (both from the testing above).
  • Three games in eight days: change at least three or four of the front six for the middle game U.
  • Use every substitution; change both No. 8s, or a No. 8 and a wide forward, around the hour U.
  • Full-backs and No. 8s burn out first: carry four full-backs and five central midfielders who can press U.
  • Keep the core stable across windows; cohesion is worth points C.
  • When the squad is running on fumes, use Control for a match U.

World-balance checks for your database

At a glance

  • Once a season, check that the non-European leagues in your database are still alive: real squads, changing champions, managers being replaced, wonderkids appearing.
  • The warning signs come from older editions L/U: grey placeholder players, vacant dug-outs, falling transfer activity and one club winning every year.
  • Leagues you run in a reduced, view-only state are the ones most at risk, so look there first.
  • Nothing stops European clubs buying the best non-European players. Your own recruitment is part of that flow.

What can go wrong, on older-edition evidence L/U: in view-only leagues grey placeholder players spread after a few seasons, managers are not replaced, transfer activity falls and one club dominates. Clubs in view-only or unloaded nations produce far fewer newgens.
